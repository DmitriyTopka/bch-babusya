# Babusya's First Sats

An animated short for the BCH BLAZE 2026 Media track.

Babusya is 81 and has never trusted a bank. For her birthday her grandson gives her a Cash Stamp, a little green card that holds real Bitcoin Cash. She thinks it's a lottery ticket. The next morning she talks the honey seller at the market into scanning one, and by lunch half the market has a wallet.

Live page: https://dmitriytopka.github.io/bch-babusya/

## Where things stand

- `index.html` is the live page. It will hold the finished film after 22 Nov.
- `start/` is the starting point frozen on 14 Nov 2026 (also tagged `start-2026-11-14`). Everything that is not in there was made during the hackathon week.
- `assets/` has the character art, the 17 chained key frames and the storyboard sheets, scaled down for the web.

Ready before 14 Nov: script, storyboard, character and location art, key frames, shot list.
Made 14 to 22 Nov: the animated shots, voice, music, wallet screens, subtitles, edit, and three 15-second cuts.

## How it is made

Frames are generated in a Pixar-like 3D style with Nano Banana on Higgsfield. Every shot is animated with Kling from a start frame to an end frame, and the end frame of one shot is the start frame of the next, so faces and props stay the same through the chain. Phone screens and QR codes are added in the edit, not by the model.
